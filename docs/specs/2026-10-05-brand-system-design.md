# TCPF Basics — brand system design

2026-10-05 · Version 1.0 · Approved via the user's review-board submission.

## Intent and agreed brief

Create a distinctive Shopify store that makes TCPF's painting-to-garment story visible and helps customers shop ready-to-wear confidently. Bespoke is a secondary offer. Keep current pricing, elevate presentation, and retain the existing logo.

The user selected **A — Contemporary Wearable Gallery** and subsequently approved this written system, including the retained gold logo, ivory/plum/periwinkle palette, Fraunces and Instrument Sans, voice, headline, and imagery mood. The user asked to continue into the phase 2 Shopify data and sample-assortment brainstorm. Product specimens remain illustrative, not verified merchandise.

The user also instructed us to replace the six existing Shopify products and their borrowed images with **ten sample products, each with 3–5 original AI-generated product photographs**. This work belongs to phase 2. Samples will be drafts with explicit concept attribution, rather than unverified merchandise offered for sale.

## Strategy

**Core idea:** Original paintings made wearable through modern Filipiniana.

**Character:** Expressive, luminous, considered, welcoming.

**Message order:** category → artist and artwork → specific garment → shopping facts → founder/bespoke exploration.

**Audience hypotheses:** occasion shoppers, expressive dressers, artist followers, and bespoke prospects. These guide questions and merchandising, not demographic claims.

The [strategy document](../brand/03-brand-strategy.md) maps the positioning, pillars, shopper jobs, competitor implications, and evidence limits. Its research foundation is in the [research findings](../brand/01-research.md) and [source register](../brand/research/source-register.md).

## Visual system

- **Logo:** client-supplied gold needle/floral mark retained intact on white. [Saved source](../brand/assets/logo/tcpf-facebook-source.jpg). Sampled gold is approximately `#CFAA66`, reserved for the logo and small decorative details.
- **Canvas/ink:** warm ivory `#F6F1E9` and deep ink `#26231F`.
- **Action color:** plum `#5D426F`, with white text.
- **Editorial accents:** periwinkle `#B7ACE0` and occasional petal `#E8BACB`, with ink text.
- **Secondary detail:** restrained leaf `#68744F`; muted text `#6B625A` on canvas.
- **Display type:** Fraunces 400/500, optical sizing automatic, softness 25, wonk 0.
- **Reading and shopping type:** Instrument Sans 400/500/600, width 100.
- **Composition:** calm product grids, controlled editorial asymmetry, purposeful artwork/garment pairings, concise art captions, tactile detail photography.

Palette text pairings were checked mathematically. Full logo integration, responsive behavior, focus states, and accessibility will need review in the actual theme. The [visual specification](../brand/05-visual-identity.md) includes measured ratios, usage rules, and locally retained font licenses.

## Voice

**An artist welcoming you into her world, with a shopkeeper's clarity about the clothing.**

Recommended headline: **“Original art. A new way to wear Filipiniana.”**

Use specific, warm artwork stories alongside plain product, size, care, price, and shipping language. The [voice guide](../brand/04-brand-voice.md) includes homepage, story, bespoke, product, and microcopy specimens. Real-product authorship copy must not be applied to generated sample paintings.

## Imagery

The [concept moodboard](../brand/assets/moodboard/wearable-gallery-v1.png) proposes soft daylight, warm gallery walls, painterly lilac/plum color, fabric texture, and structured Filipiniana detail. It is an AI direction study, not real inventory. The [generation record](../brand/assets/moodboard/provenance.json) retains the exact prompt and built-in tool attribution.

Phase 2's working target is four photographs per sample product: primary, secondary silhouette, detail, and styled outfit. Each concept will have an original artwork master and a reference photograph used for additional views. The [imagery guide](../brand/06-imagery-art-direction.md) records the sequence, consistency criteria, attribution, and upload acceptance.

## How this informs the Shopify work

Brand decisions lead into questions about artwork records, garment facts, sizing, care, set contents, related pieces, and editorial content. Those will be brainstormed before schema definitions are created. The current spec does not prescribe metaobject types or implement theme components.

The maintained [phase diagram](../diagrams/project-phases.md) shows research → brand → data/catalog → Horizon/custom theme. This existing diagram was updated for catalog replacement; an additional diagram would duplicate the same dependency structure.
