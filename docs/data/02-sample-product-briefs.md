- Size option: XS, S, M, L, XL, in that order. One colorway per product; its artwork supplies the color variation.
- Sample fabric: lightweight woven 70% cotton / 30% polyester, with no stretch. This is a hypothetical specification, not a composition inferred from an image.
- Sample lining where present: ivory 100% polyester.
- Care demonstration: gently hand wash in cold water with mild detergent; do not bleach; dry in shade; do not tumble dry; use a cool iron on the reverse. This must be replaced with manufacturer-approved instructions for real goods.
- Data status: `sample`. Artwork origin: `ai_concept`. Artwork attribution: “AI-generated sample artwork”.
- Availability: draft/unpublished; inventory tracked at zero; overselling denied. No compare-at prices.
- SKU pattern: `TCPF-SAMPLE-{UPPERCASE-HANDLE}-{SIZE}`.
- No invented model height, body measurements, customer review, celebrity credit, production origin, or dispatch deadline.

## Photo styling and acceptance

- Boleros: show the full sleeve and crop, with an ivory inner camisole. State that the inner layer is excluded.
- Skirts: full waistband-to-hem view with a simple neutral top. Include a detail that shows print and waistband.
- Sets: reuse the identical bolero and skirt designs, with no added belt or construction change.
- Halter: front view, back neckline/zip view, print/fabric detail, and a lightly styled full outfit.
- Wrap: full view, side/back showing tie placement, print/closure detail, and styled movement.
- Blouse: show the longer length, neckline, sleeves, and back fastening consistently.

All images follow the approved ivory-gallery/light-studio direction. Generated people are anonymous models, not Tisha or a celebrity. The separate artwork master counts outside the product's four photographs.
