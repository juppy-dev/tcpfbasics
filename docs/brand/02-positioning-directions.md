# TCPF Basics — positioning and creative directions

Updated: 2026-10-05. **The user selected A — Contemporary Wearable Gallery, retaining the existing logo, and approved the detailed written system.** B and C remain an archive of alternatives. Exact palette, typography, and copy are developed in the [approved brand system](../specs/2026-10-05-brand-system-design.md).

These directions answer the [research findings](01-research.md), the confirmed ready-to-wear priority, and the decision to keep current pricing while elevating the presentation. They are alternatives with different emphases, not three styles to combine indiscriminately.

## A. Contemporary wearable gallery — selected direction

**Positioning proposal:** Original paintings made wearable through modern Filipiniana.

Make Tisha's authorship visible through named artworks, garment details, and a confident editorial presentation. Keep shopping direct and approachable. Bespoke becomes an invitation to work with the artist after shoppers understand the ready-to-wear collection.

**Mood:** Expressive, luminous, considered, welcoming.

**Visual character:** A light gallery-like canvas, strong photography, expressive display typography, legible shopping type, and a small number of colors drawn from confirmed artwork. Candidate accents include periwinkle or a deeper floral tone; exact colors and fonts need development and review.

**Copy sketch:** “Original art. A new way to wear Filipiniana.” Follow immediately with a concrete explanation of printed artwork by Tisha Chavez and a clear “Shop ready-to-wear” action. This is proposed copy, not an existing brand quotation.

**Benefit:** Connects the product's specific distinction with the primary shopping goal. Can support a polished website without implying that buyers need a couture budget.

**Tradeoff:** Requires reliable artwork files and product imagery. Too much empty space or aloof language would make an accessible range feel intimidating; merchandise and price cues must stay visible.

## B. The artist's studio

**Positioning proposal:** Step into Tisha's world and take a piece of it with you.

Build more of the experience around the maker, painting process, sketchbook-like collection stories, and an intimate relationship with the audience. Product grids still carry the ready-to-wear journey.

**Mood:** Personal, tactile, romantic, curious.

**Visual character:** Paper tones, carefully used collage, painting details, informal artist notes, and pastel accents. Source imagery can support this direction; publisher collages are references rather than TCPF identity assets.

**Copy sketch:** “From my canvas to your wardrobe.” First person would require the client's agreement and an actual artist voice.

**Benefit:** Gives the founder personality and creative process a central role. Suits a community that already follows the artist.

**Tradeoff:** Needs ongoing authentic studio material and editorial maintenance. Dense collage can compete with prints and make browsing harder on mobile.

## C. Expressive Filipino occasionwear

**Positioning proposal:** Modern Filipiniana with color and character for meaningful occasions.

Lead with the wearer, silhouette, occasion, and styling. Introduce original artworks as the reason the garments look distinctive. Use a stronger campaign/editorial rhythm and clear outfit combinations.

**Mood:** Joyful, self-assured, contemporary, celebratory.

**Visual character:** Bolder typography and color blocks, campaign imagery with movement, and stronger collection/occasion merchandising.

**Copy sketch:** “Make the occasion yours.” Support it with explicit product and artwork information; the short headline is broad on its own.

**Benefit:** Makes event relevance and shopping categories immediate. Offers a natural route from attention-generating styling into an actual outfit purchase.

**Tradeoff:** Crowds into an already broad occasionwear promise and gives the artist less prominence. Celebrity-led presentation would require verified looks and should not become the only source of brand meaning.

## Decision and next step

The user selected direction A. It connects original authorship with the ready-to-wear goal. Its warmth should come from real art, language, and people; the storefront should make selecting and buying clothing straightforward.

Keep current pricing and approachable shopping central to the chosen direction. The written brand specification develops palette roles, typography, retained-logo treatment, composition, imagery, voice, and accessibility constraints. The user approved the detailed system through the review board.
