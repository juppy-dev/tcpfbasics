# VINTA Gallery — brand benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: premium atelier/bespoke benchmark; limited overlap with TCPF's primary ready-to-wear journey.

## Observed offer

The [current homepage](https://vintagallery.com/) describes the brand's move toward the Philippine market and separates made-to-measure, modifications to existing designs, and original couture. It publishes a minimum four-month made-to-measure lead time and a four-to-six-month custom timeline. A ready-to-wear clearance section is also present.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** TCPF's bespoke page can reduce ambiguity by explaining what kind of work it accepts and what inquiry information is useful. Its timelines must come from its own operations.

**Benchmark:** Distinguish standard sizing, made-to-order production, made-to-measure fit, and a new commission. These are different customer expectations.

Older indexed pages emphasize Toronto ecommerce; the current homepage takes precedence for this snapshot. Clearance prices are not used to define the brand's normal pricing.
