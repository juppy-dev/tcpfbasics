# Kultura — retail benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: multibrand shopping alternative and practical product-information benchmark.

## Observed offer

The [Filipiniana collection](https://www.kulturafilipino.com/collections/filipiniana) contains products from several brands. Observed examples include a Mayumi dress at ₱8,999 and a Raffaella cocoon top at ₱8,999. Product material, care, and model information appears within the retrieved collection/quick-view content.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** Buyers can compare a boutique with a familiar retail destination. Clear garment facts and fit support help an independent brand earn a confident purchase.

**Benchmark:** Put accurate material, care, and model/fit information near the purchase decision, while keeping the artwork story available.

The range spans multiple makers and garment types; sampled prices do not describe the whole market. No conversion or retail-performance data was measured.
