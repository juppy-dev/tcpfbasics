# Filip + Inna — brand benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: craft, provenance, and custom-client benchmark; not a direct price competitor in this study.

## Observed offer

The [About page](https://filipinna.com/pages/about) grounds the brand in weaving, embroidery, beadwork, and work with Indigenous artisans. The [custom program](https://filipinna.com/pages/filip-inna-custom) describes a relationship for developing custom pieces. No comparable garment price was captured.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** Specific process and creator information gives an emotional story substance. TCPF's corresponding evidence is the actual source painting, artist, print application, and confirmed garment construction.

**Benchmark:** Make provenance discoverable. Avoid inferring that a garment uses a traditional textile merely because it uses a Filipino silhouette.

Production and livelihood claims were not independently audited. Audience size, conversion, and SEO metrics were not measured.
