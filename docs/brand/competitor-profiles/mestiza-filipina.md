# Mestiza Filipina — brand benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: overlapping expressive modern Filipiniana shopping offer.

## Observed offer

The [Modern Filipiniana collection](https://mestizafilipina.co/en-ph/collections/modern-filipiniana) presents tops, mini dresses, skirts, trousers, boleros, and coordinated options using contemporary silhouettes. Observed examples: Nida floral top ₱6,700, its skirt ₱2,700, and Love mini dress ₱7,600. Prices refer to those items in PHP.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** Contemporary silhouettes and coordinating separates are understandable shopping benefits, but are not exclusive to TCPF. Make original artist attribution and print detail clear alongside outfit-building options.

**Benchmark:** Separate the included product from pieces used to style the image, and connect the matching pieces without implying the customer receives the whole look.

No customer demographics, conversion results, or full price distribution were measured.
