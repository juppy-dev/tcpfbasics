## Strategic interpretation

The category contains several viable promises: wearable heritage, expressive silhouettes, craft preservation, regional storytelling, and custom atelier service. TCPF's proposed anchor is more specific to its supplied business brief: **Tisha's original paintings translated into modern Filipiniana clothing**.

The opportunity is to show a verifiable relationship between each artwork and its garment while answering practical buying questions. This is a proposed direction, not proof of an uncontested market position.

A price-versus-premium positioning chart would imply a level of comparability this sample does not establish. We have not measured garment quality, margins, audience income, traffic, domain authority, rankings, or conversion. Those require separate evidence.
