# Kaayo — brand benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: regional craft and cultural-story benchmark; partial category overlap.

## Observed offer

Kaayo's [story](https://kaayoph.com/pages/about-us) identifies Mindanao, artisan relationships, and the founders as the basis of its identity. Its [homepage](https://kaayoph.com/) displays, among other items, a Marian top at ₱3,950 and a Nida dress at ₱6,500.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** A regional story is stronger when attached to actual people, places, and creative work. TCPF can develop Tisha's verified Bicol connections without borrowing the language of handweaving or artisan-community programs that has not been established for its own business.

**Benchmark:** Attribute creative contributions and name the relevant process precisely.

The brand's social-impact statements are self-published; this research did not audit outcomes. Product construction differs from TCPF, so observed prices are contextual benchmarks.
