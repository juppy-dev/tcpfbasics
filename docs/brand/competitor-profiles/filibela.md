# Filibela — brand benchmark

Research date: 2026-10-04. Relationship: overlapping modern Filipiniana ready-to-wear offer.

## Observed offer

The [homepage](https://filibela.com/) organizes shopping by garment and occasion, including work-to-evening, weddings, and travel. It displays an Aja barong at ₱3,999 and a Lovella blazer at ₱8,499. These are individual items, not a complete price range.

The [founder's story](https://filibela.com/pages/our-story-1) presents contemporary Filipino clothing as something people can comfortably wear again. The writing is personal and conversational.

## Implication for TCPF

**Inference:** Wearability is already a developed proposition in the category. TCPF should explain its particular artwork and provide equally useful garment/occasion routes. It should not assume that being modern and wearable alone makes it distinctive.

**Benchmark:** Make the next shopping step clear even when the page contains a brand story.

Traffic, conversion, customer demographics, and independently verified production claims were not measured.
