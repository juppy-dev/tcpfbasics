# TCPF Basics — brand strategy

Version 1.0 · Approved through the user's written-system review on 2026-10-05.

**Selected direction:** Contemporary Wearable Gallery. **Confirmed constraints:** retain the existing logo; ready-to-wear leads; bespoke is secondary; keep current pricing and elevate presentation.

## The central idea

**Original paintings made wearable through modern Filipiniana.**

TCPF's story starts with a specific artist and the relationship between a painting and a garment. A shopper should understand that relationship without needing to know the founder already. The presentation then helps them decide whether the piece suits their body, wardrobe, occasion, and budget.

This is a proposed articulation of the client-confirmed business. The painting-to-print transition is also supported by the [2022 founder interview](https://villagepipol.com/art-meets-fashion-tisha-chavezs-profitable-fusion/). Research does not establish exclusivity in this category: the [CCP exhibition context](https://culturalcenter.gov.ph/press-release/fashion-and-visual-arts-merge-in-ccps-filipiniana-x-obra-exhibit-at-the-national-museum-of-fine-ar/) shows a wider relationship between art and Filipiniana.

## Positioning statement

For shoppers who want modern Filipiniana with a personal point of view, TCPF Basics offers ready-to-wear clothing carrying original paintings by Tisha Chavez. Through distinctive prints, contemporary silhouettes, and an approachable shopping experience, the brand brings the artist's world into the wearer's own life.

The audience description is a strategic hypothesis. Ready-to-wear, authorship, and founder identity come from the brief; the precise customer mix requires validation.

## Purpose, ambition, and promise

These statements are recommendations for the client, rather than quotations from an existing company manifesto.

- **Purpose:** Bring original art into the way people dress and express themselves.
- **Ambition:** Become a recognizable destination for artist-led modern Filipiniana that feels personal and approachable.
- **Customer promise:** A distinctive piece, a visible creative story, and enough clear information to choose it confidently.

The promise depends on product accuracy and customer service. It does not guarantee an unconfirmed delivery date, material, fit, or bespoke result.

## Audience hypotheses and buying jobs

Avoid fixing the identity around an invented age, income, or profession. Start with the decisions a shopper is trying to make.

| Working audience | Likely job | Information that could help | Validation needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Occasion shopper | Find modern Filipiniana suitable for a particular event. | Fit, coverage, full outfit view, set contents, shipping timing. | Actual event mix and delivery questions from inquiries/orders. |
| Expressive dresser | Choose a distinctive print and a piece they can style in their own way. | Artwork story, print detail, separates, styling examples. | What repeat customers value and how they rewear purchases. |
| Existing artist follower | Find and purchase work encountered through social content. | Recognizable product names, artwork connection, direct shopping path. | Referral data and common social inquiry patterns. |
| Bespoke prospect | Understand whether a custom project is suitable. | Scope, process, examples, inquiry requirements, confirmed timing. | Current services, capacity, quote policy, and minimum lead time. |

These are hypotheses derived from the offer and category research. Indexed category anecdotes inform questions, not customer statistics. See the [research findings](01-research.md).

## Competitive choice

The six benchmarks show that wearability, cultural dress, expressive silhouettes, and craft stories already have strong voices. TCPF can give its own paintings and artist attribution more visibility while preserving clear ready-to-wear shopping.

- Filibela makes wearability and repeat use understandable: apply that clarity to styling and shopping.
- Mestiza Filipina foregrounds contemporary shapes: show exactly what a set includes and how separates work.
- Kaayo and Filip + Inna use specific provenance: name TCPF's actual creative process rather than borrowing craft claims.
- VINTA explains distinct atelier services: describe TCPF's own bespoke scope once confirmed.
- Kultura demonstrates practical product information: make art storytelling sit alongside useful facts.

These are design inferences from official sites, not findings about their sales performance. Sources and observed prices are in the [competitor comparison](competitor-profiles/_summary.md).

## Message order

1. **What it is:** Modern Filipiniana carrying original painted artworks.
2. **Why TCPF:** Paintings by Tisha Chavez, with each artwork's story made visible.
3. **Why this piece:** Its actual shape, print, materials, fit, and styling possibilities.
4. **How to buy:** Price, size selection, contents, availability, and confirmed delivery information.
5. **What else is possible:** Learn about the artist or inquire about bespoke work.

Do not make shoppers pass through a long founder story before reaching products. Do not allow the sample catalog to claim Tisha authored AI-generated concept art.

## Brand character

**Expressive:** Let paintings and color carry personality. **Considered:** Select details deliberately. **Warm:** Invite people into the story. **Assured:** Describe the product clearly. **Contemporary:** Make the clothing relevant to a current wardrobe.

Recommended balance: the imagery provides most of the emotional expression; copy and shopping controls provide confidence. A gallery metaphor describes the visual composition, not a requirement to call products “exhibits” or customers “collectors.”

## Scope and claims

The brand is based in Naga City according to the client. Use that fact naturally in the founder story. Additional regional inspiration belongs to a specific artwork when its source is confirmed; the [2025 Handuraw coverage](https://philstarlife.com/amp/article/335705-sinulid-filipino-fashion-revolution) describes a designer collection and does not establish every ready-to-wear product's story.

Celebrity appearances can support a future feature after exact looks and credits are supplied. They are not required to communicate the brand's central distinction. Sustainability, artisan partnerships, handmade production, scarcity, stock, and customer reviews require their own evidence.

## Success criteria for the brand work

- A new visitor can identify the product category and painting-to-garment relationship in the opening message.
- The existing logo remains intact and recognizable.
- Prices and shopping actions feel consistent with an approachable ready-to-wear offer.
- Voice, imagery, and composition carry the same warm, artistic character.
- The client can apply the palette, type, copy, and image rules to new content.
- Product facts and generated concepts are distinguishable throughout the demonstration build.

These criteria guide design review. Conversion impact will require post-launch measurement.
